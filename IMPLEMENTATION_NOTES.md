# Implementation notes

M1–M5 are implemented; see `PROGRESS.md` for completion evidence. One 2000 m Harbor Loop, three deterministic opponents and three valid player laps form the complete race. `SPEC.md`, dependency pins and lockfile are unchanged. Audio/final art polish (M6) and release/performance acceptance (M7) remain pending.

- Strict TypeScript, Canvas 2D and semantic HTML/CSS; original procedural art, no framework, runtime service, hosted asset or external API. The pure `src/game/` modules receive explicit state, input, track and timestep.
- Simulation runs at 60 Hz with integer ticks and fractional swept crossing timestamps for lap times and finish order. Rendering interpolates unwrapped positions. Blur/hidden/exceptional gaps above 250 ms pause, clear input and rebase; explicit resume preserves countdown/racing state.
- Harbor Loop has five eased bends, wide passing straights, visible boundaries/start line and three collidable tire stacks. Outward load grows with curvature and speed squared; braking beats excessive speed at Anchor. Grass reduces grip/speed. Swept car/world contacts and lateral separation preserve station and allow recovery. Constants live in `src/game/config.ts`.
- Azure/Gold/Violet share physics with small deterministic performance differences. Lookahead, safe lane selection, passing commitment and following avoid oscillation/stalls without rubber banding, randomness or teleports. Their repeated ten-traversal regression remains intact.
- Four pre-line slots remain stationary for 180 countdown ticks. The first crossing arms only; ordered quarter gates and a physically bounded lawful station reject skipped/reverse/discontinuous progress. Normal off-road driving remains legal. All crossings are processed before taking immediate frozen player results after lap three.
- Replay resets race clocks, grid, AI, contacts and gates. The controller retains the personal-record adapter and clears held input/rebases the animation loop. Test-only keyboard drivers live under `tests/` and never enter the production bundle.
- M5 stores only improved, valid completed player-lap events, even before results. Version 1 JSON uses finite positive fractional milliseconds in `localStorage`; corrupt/unknown records are ignored. Storage getter/read/write failures keep an improving session record and a concise saving-unavailable indication. Re-reading before comparison respects an already better record from another tab, without claiming atomic cross-tab locking. Browser policy or cleared site data can prevent retention.
- RACE BEST labels current-race completed laps; PERSONAL BEST labels the saved/session record. Replay resets only race best. The menu/results and external record strip show personal best when present. UI initializes synchronously, formats ordinal positions and millisecond clocks, and provides visible start/resume/replay focus with native Enter activation. The documented minimum is 960×720; 1280×800 or larger is preferred.

Verification is consolidated in `VERIFICATION.md`: type/lint/logic/build, complete keyboard race/persistence/replay/reload, corrupt/denied storage, compatibility and visual review. Exact equal full-race schedules at 30/60/120/144 Hz, M2 braking comparison and M3 traffic bounds are retained. Native Chrome supplements automated keyboard/UI checks; multi-key holding is covered by Playwright. No full-release performance or audio acceptance is claimed.

The existing M1 audit reported 11 moderate development-tool minimatch findings and zero production findings; unchanged dependencies were not re-audited. No M5 scope change or new dependency. Leave changes for user review/commit and stop after M5.
